# 📄 Investigation Objectives

The primary objective of **Operation Shadow Drop** is to analyze and document a simulated cybersecurity incident through network traffic investigation and digital evidence analysis.

The investigation aims to identify attacker activities, understand the attack methodology, determine the impact on the affected environment, and provide recommendations to improve security defenses.

***

### Primary Objectives

#### 1. Analyze Network Traffic

Examine the provided packet capture (PCAP) file to identify suspicious network activity, malicious communications, and potential indicators of compromise.

***

#### 2. Identify Attacker Activity

Determine the actions performed by the attacker, including:

* Initial access attempts.
* Malicious requests.
* Unauthorized activities.
* Post-compromise behavior.

***

#### 3. Determine Attack Indicators

Identify important technical evidence associated with the attack, including:

* Attacker IP address.
* Geographic origin.
* User-Agent information.
* Malicious files or scripts.
* Communication channels.

***

#### 4. Reconstruct the Attack Timeline

Develop a timeline showing the sequence of events from initial attacker interaction through attempted data access or exfiltration activities.

***

#### 5. Assess Impact

Evaluate the potential impact of the attack, including:

* Affected systems.
* Potentially compromised data.
* Security weaknesses exploited by the attacker.

***

#### 6. Provide Security Recommendations

Develop practical recommendations to help prevent similar incidents, improve monitoring capabilities, and strengthen the organization's security posture.

***

### Investigation Scope

This investigation focuses on:

* Network packet analysis.
* Web application attack analysis.
* Attacker behavior identification.
* Digital evidence examination.
* Incident response documentation.

The investigation does not include remediation activities or system recovery actions, as the focus is analysis and reporting of the simulated incident.
